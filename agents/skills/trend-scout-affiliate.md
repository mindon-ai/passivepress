# Affiliate Trend Scout

Find product topics with buying intent.

Prioritize:
- Amazon Best Sellers and movers by category.
- Queries like "best X 2026", "best X under $N", "X vs Y", "X review".
- Reddit buying communities and problem/solution threads.
- Independent review-site signals from RTINGS, Wirecutter, TechRadar, PCMag, and The Verge.

Score by search volume, buying intent, product specificity, freshness, and duplicate avoidance. Return TrendTopic objects with niche, contentType, productHints, searchVolume, score, and sourceUrls when possible.
